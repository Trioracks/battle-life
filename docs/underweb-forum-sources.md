# Historical forum reading archive

The local **Подземка** discussions are original fiction. Their nicks, messages,
dates, and signatures are game content. The separate **Hip-Hop.ru / архив**
is a curated, read-only selection of observed historical posts. It does not
claim to reproduce the complete threads or the exact 2012 website layout.

Each archived row preserves an observed author nickname, date, time, and source
page URL. Editorial summaries are explicitly labelled **Пересказ сообщения**
and use third-person descriptions, never invented dialogue attributed to real
people. Excerpts are explicitly labelled **Короткая цитата из источника**.
Copied emoticon codes, nested quotes, signatures, unrelated page furniture and
full posts are omitted. All excerpts from any one source webpage total fewer
than 25 words. Summaries from any one webpage stay below 200 words.

Verified with primary-source search results on 2026-10-11 (direct page fetches
initially returned cache misses; indexed content provided the dated posts).
The indexed primary-page text includes the author/date/time/body of every
selected message; it is not used to infer unseen replies. A subsequent direct
HTTP check of the RE-pac page returned a connection-security challenge, not
the thread. The current live site could not be independently compared to the
indexed text during this verification.

Page navigation is local selection pagination (six selected messages per page).
It is labelled as such and is not presented as the original forum's post
numbering. Source links open the actual source page.

Game day 1 is 1 September 2012. A topic exists in the game archive only once at
least one of its selected posts has a publication date at or before the game
date. Its individual posts follow the same rule. Consequently the microphone
selection appears on day 32, gains its 4 October replies on day 34, and the
album-year discussion appears on day 61. No archive reply form is available,
and reading historical material does not modify local replies or drafts.

The board taxonomy and visual design are an honest period-inspired
reconstruction. The current Hip-Hop.ru homepage is not used as evidence for
the site's exact 2012 layout or category structure.

## Save compatibility

All twelve original local battle topic IDs and their numbered seeded post IDs
remain valid. Local reply IDs, quoted post IDs, drafts, and read dates are
normalized with the existing save format. New local boards use new IDs. The
historical archive is not part of the local reply/draft namespace.
